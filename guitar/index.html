Chord Trainer

Play real chords on your own guitar as they reach the line. The microphone judges each chord by its sound. Turn the camera on and it learns what your hand looks like for each chord you get right, then tells you whether your fingers are in the shape before you strum.

The mic is off. Nothing is judged until it is on. Headphones help.

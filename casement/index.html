Lites in this window

Photo

Stand square to the window or door and get all of it in the frame. A photo has no scale of its own, so give it one: enter one tape reading afterwards for the best result, or tape a sheet of letter paper flat against what you are measuring for a rough figure with no tape at all.

Scale from

Upper drawing

The frame. Worked out as screen opening + hinge offset across, + nose offset up. That rule reproduces the 10 1/4 and 11 printed on the blank sheet.

Lower drawing

The screen opening, A. Measure one width and one height and the rest of this window fills in.

Plane table

Enter A in any column, or use the top row to feed a column from the drawing. The sheet says A = B, so B and the five planes below fill in. Measure B too and the difference gets checked.

Laser

A laser in keyboard mode types straight into the keypad, in inches, millimetres or metres. Connect a Leica DISTO over Bluetooth here, on Chrome. This has not been tried on a real one yet.

Plumb and bow

+ measurement means out of plumb or bowed to the interior of the window and − means out of plumb or bowed to the exterior of the window.

Seals and keeper

The sheet gives no offset for these four, so nothing here is ever calculated.

    What the photo of the sheet could not settle
    • Columns A to H are not defined on this page. The app keeps them as plain columns, and each one works out on its own.
    • There are two section drawings with different offsets. The left one's depths (4 1/2 in, 2 3/8 in) match Kohltech's published 2020 Supreme casement drawing, so they are probably two product lines. The right-hand set is the default because it reproduces the printed drawings. Pick the one for the window in front of you.
    • The tolerance after "A = B" is too blurry to read in the photo. Enter it from the paper and A against B gets a pass or fail.
    • The left-hand keeper offset is also too blurry. It looks like 13/16 and stays blank until it is entered.
    • The last legend line reads "Keeper reach" as best the photo shows.
    • A photo measures the plane of the opening. Seals, keeper height and reach, overall height, plumb and bow can't be seen from the front, so they stay for the tape and the level.

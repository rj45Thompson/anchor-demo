Blueprint City

Start small: a Pong ball and paddle (32 nodes, UE 4.18, anonymous), then siljaZ's asteroid shooter (16 pastes, 232 nodes, UE 5.0) - both playing on their converted code - then Lock-On System by hitted (blueprintue.com, 162 nodes, UE 5.0), and last a whole enemy AI by JJ GameDesign (blueprintue.com, 1,009 nodes, UE 5.3): each converted to code and traced to the requirements it serves. The authors keep the rights, and any author's graphs come down on request.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, W A S D to fly (while a game plays, the keys it names are its own); point at anything to name it, click it for what it is, or press Mic and say a command. Unreal 2D shows the selected graph's paste the way the Blueprint editor draws it.

Read the whole Blueprint as code: every class, field and method of the set shown, not just one node.

A draft prototype by RJ Thompson, built with AI agents he directed, and measured by tools: no person has tested it yet. The samples are public pastes on blueprintue.com: Pong (anonymous, original), an asteroid shooter (siljaZ) and the Lock-On System (hitted, original). Their authors keep the rights; the graphs here are derived from them, and any comes down at its author’s request. The prototype’s source is open, under the MIT licence: github.com/rj45Thompson/blueprint-view-source.

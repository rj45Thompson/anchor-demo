How you play
  • Five frets come down five lanes. Press the matching button as a gem crosses the white line.
  • On a phone: d-pad left, up, right are the first three lanes, X and A the last two. Y spends star power.
  • On this keyboard: 12345 or ASDFG, and space for star power.
  • Hold a long gem to keep it scoring. Ten notes in a row doubles your score, and it climbs to four times.
  • The singer watches the band at the top and sings the line. An octave out still counts.

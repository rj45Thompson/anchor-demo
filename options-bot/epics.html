EPIC-1 · epic

Trade one day of SPY 0DTE opening-range breakouts

Actor
Trader
Trigger
the trader starts the bot before or during a trading day
Before
TWS or IB Gateway runs with the API on PORT; the account has live SPY and US options data
Succeeds when
each breakout of the day is traded within the trader's limits, its exits held at IB, and logged as one row
At worst
no order or position of the bot's is left behind, and every trade it made is in the log
Why
the original script's purpose: the 15-minute opening range breakout on SPY 0DTE options, sized to a small balance

How the trader acts: runs python zdt_autopilot.py, with --live for a live account, and presses Ctrl+C to stop

How they see the result: the console's tagged lines ([SIGNAL], [FILLED], [TRADE CLOSED]) and trade_execution_log.csv

Steps, each a use case below

  1. The trader starts the bot, and it connects to the right kind of account. UC-1: Start the bot on the right account
    IR-1OB-1
  2. The bot reads the opening range and prints the two triggers. UC-2: Read the opening range
    OB-2
  3. The bot watches SPY until it breaks out past a trigger. UC-3: Watch SPY for a breakout
    OB-3
  4. The bot buys today's out-of-the-money option, sized to the balance. UC-4: Enter a trade on the signal
    OB-4OB-5
  5. IB holds the stop and the target until one fills, or the bot flattens before the close. UC-5: Exit the trade
    IR-3IR-4
  6. The bot logs the trade and decides whether to take another. UC-6: Log the trade and decide whether to go on
    OB-8OB-7
  7. The bot stops and leaves nothing of its own working or held. UC-7: Stop and leave nothing behind
    IR-10

Alternate paths

  • 1a When the exit rules are not set: The bot runs SIGNALS ONLY: steps 2 to 4 print the order it would send, and no order is sent. Ends: nothing is left behind (the minimal guarantee).
    IR-2
  • 2a When IB lists today as closed: The bot says so and stops. Ends: nothing is left behind (the minimal guarantee).
    IR-5

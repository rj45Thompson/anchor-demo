Options trader bot

It buys a same-day (0DTE) SPY option on Interactive Brokers when SPY breaks out of its first 15 minutes. Safe by default: no orders until you set a stop and a target, and paper only unless you add --live.

What it does

  1. Takes the high and low of the first 15 minutes.
  2. CALL when SPY goes $0.30 above that range, PUT when $0.30 below.
  3. Buys today's option about $1.50 out of the money, sized to its budget ($100 to start).
  4. Leaves the stop and target at IB, and logs each trade to a CSV.
  5. Stops for the day after two losses in a row.

All 13 steps, and what can go wrong

Fixed from the original

  • Bought a contract it could not afford.
  • Read the wrong bar as the opening range.
  • Kept trading on a made-up $100 when the balance failed to load.
  • Switched to tomorrow's expiry, which is not 0DTE.
  • Crashed when no strike was near, and used the PC's clock instead of New York's.

Checked against its spec

The light loop held it to 19 rules from its written spec and proved 3 faults: a wrong strike on a tie, an in-the-money buy, and a log row lost on Ctrl+C. All three are fixed.

Tested

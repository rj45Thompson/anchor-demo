Options trader bot

On Interactive Brokers, it buys a SPY option that expires the same day (0DTE) when SPY breaks out of its first 15 minutes. Finished from a script that arrived cut off, then checked and tested.

Run it

  2. pip install -r requirements.txt
  3. python zdt_autopilot.py

Needs live SPY and options data. Setup in the manual

What it does

  1. Takes the high and low of the first 15 minutes.
  2. CALL when SPY goes $0.30 above that range, PUT when $0.30 below.
  3. Buys today's option about $1.50 out of the money, sized to its budget ($100 to start).
  4. Leaves the stop and target at IB, and logs each trade to a CSV.
  5. Stops for the day after two losses in a row.

Fixed from the original

  • Bought a contract it could not afford.
  • Read the wrong bar as the opening range.
  • Kept trading on a made-up $100 when the balance failed to load.
  • Switched to tomorrow's expiry, which is not 0DTE.
  • Crashed when no strike was near, and used the PC's clock instead of New York's.

How others do it

  • Hugging Face: no model or dataset for this strategy. The nearest data is 5-minute option bars.
  • Tools: QuantConnect trades options through IB; NautilusTrader connects to IB and loads option chains.
  • Research: breakout studies on shares report gains; a 0DTE version sells spreads instead of buying.
  • 0DTE: retail traders lost about $70 million in 2021 to 2023; one study's profits vanished once spreads were charged in full.
  • AI agents: research frameworks trade in simulation; a 20-year test found their reported edge shrinks.
